Feature · Insights

Your habits have patterns.
The AI Analyser finds them.

Streaks tell you whether you showed up. The AI Habit Analyser tells you why you did, and why you didn't. It reads your history quietly and surfaces what it notices, without lectures or fake enthusiasm.

Free to download · iOS 15+ · No account required

How It Works

What "AI analysis" actually means here

There's a lot of AI washing in productivity apps right now, features that are called "AI" but are really just a static motivational quote. The AI Habit Analyser in Productify is different: it reads your actual completion data across time, identifies real patterns, and generates observations that are specific to your behaviour.

It might notice that you complete your morning meditation 94% of the time on weekdays, but only 41% on weekends, and surface that asymmetry for you to consider. It might observe that your water intake habit dips significantly in the week before your monthly review at work. These are patterns a streak counter can't show you. They require looking across time at the context around your completions and misses.

Meet TempO, your AI companion. TempO (Temp Owl) is Productify's AI character, a calm, thoughtful presence who delivers insights without drama. No aggressive nudges, no guilt-inducing language. Just honest observations, offered gently, that help you understand yourself a little better.

What the analyser looks for

Day-of-week patterns

Your habits don't perform equally across all seven days. Most people have a significantly different completion rate on weekdays versus weekends, and within the working week, certain days (often Wednesdays or Fridays) tend to be weaker. The analyser maps this and can tell you your strongest and most vulnerable days, useful information for deciding whether to lower your target on certain days or protect your routine more deliberately.

Streak recovery behaviour

How you respond after breaking a streak is more predictive of long-term success than the streak length itself. The analyser tracks your "return rate", how quickly you get back to the habit after a missed day. If your return rate is high (you almost always come back the next day), that's a sign of resilience worth noticing. If you tend to abandon habits after a first miss, that's a pattern worth addressing.

Habit cluster correlations

If you track multiple habits, the analyser looks for correlations between them. It might notice that on days when you exercise, you're also significantly more likely to complete your evening reading habit, or that skipping sleep consistently correlates with skipping morning habits the following day. These connections, once visible, give you useful leverage points.

Progress trajectory

Beyond current streak, the analyser calculates your rolling 30-day and 90-day completion rate per habit, so you can see whether a habit is genuinely getting stronger over time, or whether a good recent streak is masking a longer-term plateau. A rising trajectory is meaningful. A flat one is information.

What the analyser doesn't do

  • It doesn't shame you for missed days or use language designed to induce guilt
  • It doesn't make predictions about your future: only observations about your past
  • It doesn't send push notifications demanding you act on its findings
  • It doesn't invent patterns that aren't there: it only reports what the data shows
  • It doesn't replace your own judgement: insights are offered, never prescribed

Identifying weak spots

Find the specific days or contexts where your habits consistently slip, so you can plan around them rather than being surprised by them.

🔁

Understanding recovery

See how quickly you return after breaks. A fast recovery rate matters more than an unbroken streak for sustainable long-term habits.

🔗

Finding keystone habits

Discover which habits, when completed, tend to pull other habits along with them. Focus your energy on the ones that do the most work.

📈

Measuring real progress

A 90-day completion rate tells you more than a current streak. Watch your rate climb over months and you'll see the actual evidence of change.

The AI Habit Analyser is available after you've built up a few weeks of data. The more history Productify has, the more specific and useful its observations become. It works quietly in the background, you open it when you're ready to reflect, not because it demanded your attention.
