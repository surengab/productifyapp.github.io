## What are Habit Templates?

Habit Templates are pre-built habit configurations you can add to your tracker in seconds. Each template includes:
- A habit name and description
- A recommended frequency and scheduling
- A suggested reminder time
- A goal type (binary or measurable with a suggested target)

Instead of starting from a blank screen and wondering what to track, you pick from a curated library and customise from there.

## Why templates matter

The blank slate is one of the biggest barriers to starting a habit tracker. When you open a new app and see "Add your first habit" with no guidance, the cognitive load of deciding *what* to track stops many people before they start.

Templates eliminate that barrier. You're starting from a curated, tested habit rather than an empty text field.
