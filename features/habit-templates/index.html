Feature · Getting Started

Don't start from scratch.
Start from a template.

The hardest part of building a new routine is often the blank page. What habit should I track? How should I measure it? How often? Productify's template library answers those questions for you, so you can start your first day today, not next week.

Free to download · iOS 15+ · No account required

How It Works

Why the setup moment matters so much

Behavioural research is consistent on one thing: the longer the gap between intent and first action, the less likely the action is to happen. When you decide you want to "drink more water" but then have to spend 15 minutes configuring a habit from scratch, what to call it, what unit to track, what the daily target should be, what time to set a reminder, you burn through decision energy before the habit has even started.

Templates collapse that setup time to a few seconds. A "Daily Hydration" template comes pre-configured with a sensible goal (8 glasses), a trackable unit (glasses), and an optional reminder time. You can accept it as-is or adjust any part of it. Either way, you're logging your first check-in within a minute of deciding to start.

The "activation energy" principle, drawn from James Clear's Atomic Habits, says that reducing the friction at the start of a behaviour is often more effective than increasing your motivation. Templates are friction-reduction applied directly to the habit setup process.

What's in the template library

Productify's templates are organised by life area, so you can browse by what you're trying to improve rather than scrolling through an undifferentiated list.

Health & body

Hydration, sleep schedule, steps, stretching, medication reminders, no-alcohol days, and more. Each comes with a pre-set measurement type and a goal that's challenging but realistic, based on widely used health guidelines, not aspirational marketing.

Mind & focus

Meditation, journaling, deep work blocks, reading, screen-free evenings, gratitude practice. These habits are configured as time-based or binary (done / not done), depending on which measurement approach tends to produce better adherence.

Work & learning

Language practice, skill study, writing sessions, code practice, review cycles. For learners and builders who want to track consistent effort rather than outcome, because consistent effort is the variable you can control.

Routines & rituals

Morning routines, evening wind-downs, weekly reviews. These templates are designed to stack naturally with each other, so you can build a coherent daily routine rather than a disconnected list of habits.

How templates work in Productify

  • Browse by category or search by keyword: find what you need in under 30 seconds
  • Every template ships with a name, measurement type, default goal, and optional reminder
  • Every field is editable: rename it, change the target, adjust the frequency
  • Templates don't lock you in: you can modify any habit at any time without losing your history
  • Add as many or as few as you need: there's no limit, though starting with one or two is almost always better

Templates are a starting point, not a prescription. The best habit is the one you'll actually do, so don't hesitate to modify a template until it fits your life, not the other way around. Productify makes it easy to start with a sensible default and then adjust as you learn what actually works for you.
