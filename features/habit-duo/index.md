## What is Habit Duo?

Habit Duo is Productify's unique two-person accountability feature. It pairs you with one specific friend or partner on one specific habit. Both of you track it separately, and both of you can see each other's progress.

It's deliberately focused:
- **One person, not a group.** No leaderboards, no social pressure from strangers.
- **One habit, not your whole routine.** Focused accountability is more effective than broad accountability.
- **Private, not public.** Only you and your Habit Duo partner see each other's data.

---

## Why accountability works (and why most apps do it wrong)

Research consistently shows that accountability significantly improves habit adherence. But the type of accountability matters:

- **Social pressure** (public dashboards, group leaderboards) creates anxiety and can backfire — particularly for people who respond badly to comparison
- **Focused, private accountability** (one trusted person, one shared goal) produces better outcomes with less stress

Most habit apps either have no accountability features or implement group/social accountability with gamification and competition. Habit Duo is designed specifically around the focused, private model.

---

## How it works

1. Choose one habit you want to build with an accountability partner
2. Invite one friend via the Habit Duo feature in Productify
3. Both of you track the habit separately in your own apps
4. Both of you can see whether the other checked in each day
5. The shared visibility creates gentle, daily accountability without pressure

## Best use cases

- Building an exercise habit with a friend who has the same goal
- Accountability for a creative habit (writing, drawing) with a partner
- Morning routine consistency with a partner or spouse
- Study habits with a friend or colleague
- Any habit where knowing someone else sees your daily check-in is motivating
