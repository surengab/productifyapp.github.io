Productify's smart reminders are gentle, schedulable habit cues. Customise each reminder independently for maximum effectiveness — without notification fatigue.

---

## How smart reminders work

In Productify, every habit has its own reminder settings:

- **Per-habit scheduling** — each habit fires at its own time, independent of others
- **Day-of-week control** — set reminders only for days the habit is scheduled
- **Optional** — not all habits need reminders; some are best left cue-free

When the reminder fires, it acts as the designed **cue** in the habit loop: the trigger that initiates the routine.

---

## Why per-habit reminders matter

A single daily "check your habits" notification trains you to ignore it. When you open the app and see 8 incomplete habits at 8pm, the cognitive load discourages action.

Per-habit reminders are contextual:
- "Walk reminder" fires at lunchtime
- "Meditation reminder" fires at 7:30am
- "Reading reminder" fires at 9pm

Each notification arrives at the right moment for that specific habit, dramatically increasing the probability of completing it.

---

## Timing reminders for maximum effectiveness

The most effective reminder time is **immediately after an anchor habit** — an existing behaviour you already do automatically.

Examples:
- Set your journaling reminder 10 minutes after your usual wake time (after you've made coffee)
- Set your reading reminder 5 minutes before your usual bedtime
- Set your walk reminder at the time you typically take your lunch break

This turns the reminder into a habit stack cue, linking the new habit to an existing routine.

---

## Avoiding notification fatigue

- **Don't set reminders for every habit** — habits with strong natural cues (morning water, bedtime reading) often don't need reminders
- **Space reminders at least 30 minutes apart** — bunched notifications feel like harassment
- **Review and remove reminders** — once a habit becomes automatic, the reminder is no longer needed and can distract from newer habits that still need the nudge
