# What Habits Should I Track? 30 Daily Habits Worth Building

**URL:** https://productifyapp.org/blog/what-habits-to-track/  
**Type:** Habit ideas guide  
**Published:** April 2026

Not sure what to put in your habit tracker? Here are 30 daily habits worth building, organised by category — health, focus, mindset, relationships, and more.

---

## How to choose which habits to track

Before looking at the list, apply this filter:

1. **Start with one or two habits, not ten.** Every additional habit reduces the probability of any of them sticking.
2. **Choose habits you actually want, not habits you think you should want.** Extrinsic obligation creates resentment.
3. **Make them daily or near-daily.** Habits that happen once a week are much harder to automate.
4. **Make them specific.** "Exercise" is not a trackable habit. "Walk for 20 minutes after lunch" is.

---

## Health habits (physical)

1. **Drink a glass of water on waking** — Simple, immediate, and one of the highest-impact health habits available.
2. **Walk for 20 minutes** — Daily walking is associated with improved cardiovascular health, mood, and sleep quality.
3. **Do 10 minutes of movement before breakfast** — Bodyweight exercises, stretching, or yoga. Gets blood moving before the day's demands hit.
4. **Track water intake (8 glasses)** — Use a measurable goal in Productify to track individual glasses.
5. **No screens 1 hour before bed** — Reduces blue light exposure and significantly improves sleep onset time.
6. **Eat a vegetable with every meal** — Simple and specific. Easier to track than broader nutrition goals.
7. **Take a 5-minute walk after sitting for 1 hour** — Counteracts the metabolic effects of prolonged sitting.
8. **Strength training (3x per week)** — Track as a weekly frequency habit rather than daily.

---

## Focus and deep work habits

9. **Write your top 3 priorities for the day before opening email** — Protects the first 15 minutes of your working day from reactive work.
10. **One 25-minute focused work block (no interruptions)** — A minimal version of deep work. Use the Pomodoro Technique as a starting point.
11. **No social media before 10am** — Protects morning cognitive resources from the reactive state that social media induces.
12. **Close all tabs and notifications during focus blocks** — Environment design for the working context.
13. **End the workday with a brief review (5 minutes)** — What did you complete? What's on tomorrow? Creates a clean cognitive end to the day.

---

## Mindset and wellbeing habits

14. **Journaling (1 sentence minimum)** — The two-minute rule applied to reflective writing. One sentence is better than nothing. Extend over time.
15. **Meditation (5 minutes)** — Start shorter than you think you need. Consistency matters more than duration.
16. **Gratitude (3 things)** — Research associates daily gratitude practice with improved mood and reduced stress. Takes under 3 minutes.
17. **Read 10 pages** — Daily reading compounds dramatically. 10 pages/day = ~18 books a year.
18. **No alcohol on weekdays** — A measurable and high-impact health habit for many people.
19. **Spend 10 minutes outside** — Light exposure, fresh air, and physical separation from screens all have evidence behind them.

---

## Sleep habits

20. **Consistent wake time (7 days a week)** — Sleep researchers consistently identify this as the single highest-impact sleep habit. Even on weekends.
21. **In bed by [time]** — Pair with wake time for full circadian consistency.
22. **Phone out of the bedroom** — Removes the temptation to check it before sleep or during night waking.
23. **Wind-down routine (20 minutes before bed)** — A consistent pre-sleep routine acts as a cue that tells your nervous system it's time to sleep.

---

## Relationship and social habits

24. **Send one meaningful message to a friend or family member** — Connection maintenance. Takes 2 minutes and compounds in relationship quality.
25. **Call or voice note someone (not text)** — Higher-quality connection than text for maintaining close relationships.

---

## Learning and creative habits

26. **Language practice (10 minutes)** — Apps like Duolingo are designed around this. Track it as a separate habit for accountability outside the app.
27. **Learn one new thing per day** — Could be an article, a podcast segment, or a Wikipedia rabbit hole. Specific enough to be actionable.
28. **Creative work (15 minutes)** — Writing, drawing, music, photography — anything creative. The constraint of 15 minutes removes the excuse of "I don't have time."
29. **Review notes or flashcards (5 minutes)** — Spaced repetition for anything you're learning.

---

## Financial habits

30. **Review your spending for the day (2 minutes)** — Awareness of where money goes is the foundation of any financial habit. Use a banking app or simple note.

---

## How many habits should you track?

| Stage | Recommended habits |
|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | 1–2 habits only |
| Week 3–8 | 2–3 habits (add one only after first is feeling automatic) |
| Month 3+ | 3–5 habits maximum |
| Established system | 5–7 habits comfortably |

Most habit researchers, including James Clear, recommend against tracking more than 5–7 habits simultaneously regardless of experience level. Fewer habits tracked consistently beats more habits tracked sporadically.
