The free plan

What you actually get for free

Plenty of habit trackers describe themselves as free and then cap you at three habits or hide reminders behind a subscription. Productify's free plan covers a real routine: five habits, unlimited history, per-habit daily tracking, and streak tracking that survives a missed day.

  • No account required: install the app and start tracking; your data stays on your device by default
  • No time limit: the free plan is not a trial that expires after a week
  • No ads: the free tier is funded by people who upgrade, not by advertising
  • Reminders included: per-habit notifications are part of the free plan, not a paid add-on
Five habits is usually enough for a morning routine or a focused set of daily targets. If you are tracking a whole week's worth of areas (see our guide to areas of life), that is where the habit limit starts to bite.
Upgrading

When Pro is worth paying for

Pro is not a better version of the same thing. It adds capabilities the free plan does not have at all. It is worth it if one of these describes you:

  • You want to track more than five habits at once
  • You want Habit Duo: one shared habit with one friend and private, side-by-side streak progress
  • You want the AI Habit Analyser to surface which cues and days actually predict your slips
  • You want measurable goals in custom units rather than simple yes/no check-ins
  • You want the full habit template library rather than the five starters

If none of those apply, the free plan is genuinely the right choice, and we would rather you stayed on it than paid for something you do not use.

Monthly or yearly?

At the currently listed US prices, yearly costs less than twelve monthly renewals. Monthly keeps the initial commitment smaller while you decide whether Pro solves a real problem. Promotional offers and eligibility vary, so compare the duration and renewal terms on Apple's purchase sheet rather than choosing from the headline price alone.
