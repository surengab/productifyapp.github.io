About Productify

Productify started as a personal project, a distraction-free tool for iOS rooted in Atomic Habits-style thinking: small, measurable actions done with intention over time. It's grown entirely on user feedback, and the core philosophy hasn't changed: calm, clear, and built for real people living real lives.
