Privacy Policy

How Productify handles your data — clearly, and without legal jargon.

Last updated: April 2026

About This Policy

DEDUCTIFY LLC built the Productify app as a commercial app. This service is provided by DEDUCTIFY LLC and is intended for use as is.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app. If you choose to use Productify, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described here.

1. Information Handling

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Productify is designed with a privacy-first approach. Habit titles, schedules, completion data, and AI conversation history are stored locally on your device and are not collected or stored on our servers.

Information Sent Only When You Use the AI Assistant

The app includes an optional AI habit coaching assistant ("TempO") powered by the OpenAI GPT API. If you choose to use this feature and request AI analysis, the content you submit may be transmitted securely to OpenAI solely to generate a response.

What may be sent to OpenAI:

  • Messages you type in the AI assistant
  • Habit information only when you explicitly request analysis

What we do not send:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your account credentials

OpenAI processes data according to its own privacy practices: openai.com/privacy

Automatically Processed Technical Information

Some limited technical information such as device model, operating system version, IP address, and diagnostic data may be processed temporarily to maintain secure communication and improve app stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Generate AI responses when requested
  • Maintain performance, security, and reliability

We do not sell personal information.

3. Service Providers

We may rely on trusted third-party providers, including OpenAI, to operate certain features. These providers process information only to perform tasks on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Most data remains stored locally on your device. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the service.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have control over your information:

  • You may disable the AI assistant at any time
  • You may delete app data directly from your device
  • You may contact us with privacy-related questions

6. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates become effective when posted within the app or on our website. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
