About

The studio behind Productify

Productify is made by Deductify, a small, independent product studio that has been shipping software since 2018. We build our own apps alongside work for clients, which keeps us accountable for the quality of both.

Who we are

Deductify (legally DEDUCTIFY, LLC) designs and builds its own iOS apps, and engineers connected-device software for other companies, from firmware to the phone in someone’s hand. We are a small team. Productify is one of our own products, and messages about it go straight to the people who build it.

Why we build Productify

Productify started as a personal project: a distraction-free habit tracker for iPhone, rooted in the idea of small, measurable actions done with intention over time. It has grown on user feedback, and the core belief has not changed. A routine practiced imperfectly for years is worth more than a perfect system abandoned in weeks, so the app should work with you rather than turn every missed day into a verdict.

How we build it

  • No account to start. Install the app and begin. Your habit data stays on your device by default.
  • A free plan that doesn’t expire. Up to 5 habits with reminders and streaks, with no trial clock. Pro adds unlimited habits, Habit Duo and AI insights.
  • Missed days are information, not failure. A missed day resets the current streak, but your full history stays visible.
  • Honest guides. Our articles cite their sources, disclose that we make Productify, and label suggestions as suggestions. Read our editorial standards.
  • Available in 11 languages, on iPhone and Apple Watch, with widgets.
