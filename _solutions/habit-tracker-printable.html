How to print and use your tracker

  1. Match the paper. Select A4 for 210 × 297 mm paper, or US Letter for 8.5 × 11 inches. The monthly page is landscape; weekly pages are portrait.
  2. Check print preview. Use actual size or 100%, with one page per sheet. If your printer clips an edge, select “Fit to printable area.” The sheets work in black and white.
  3. Choose an action and a schedule. Write “read 10 minutes, weekdays” instead of “read more.” Put a dash on days when the habit is not planned.
  4. Record what happened. Tick a completed action, write an amount, or leave a missed session blank. Add a note if you forgot to record it.
  5. Review your planned days. Notice what got in the way and change one thing, such as the target or time, for next week.

Paper or a habit tracker app?

Paper gives you a record you can keep on your desk, fridge, or inside a notebook. You can tally completion and spot patterns yourself. An app can add reminders and progress views when you want them.
