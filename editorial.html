About our guides

Productify Editorial Team

The publishing team behind Productify’s guides to habit tracking, daily routines, and using our app.

Who publishes these articles

Productify publishes this blog to help readers turn useful ideas into practical routines. “Productify Editorial Team” is our shared publishing byline. It does not imply that every article is written by a scientist or independently reviewed by a clinician.

How to read our sources

Research citations should link to the work being discussed and support the specific claim beside them. A study result describes its participants, methods, and context; it is not a guarantee for an individual reader. Popular habit-building frameworks are identified as practical models rather than treated as clinical evidence.

Worked examples and planning suggestions are labeled when they are illustrative. They should not be read as measured customer results.

Product comparisons and commercial interests

We make Productify and have a commercial interest in its success. Our comparisons include that disclosure and link to the official listings used to describe other products. Recommendations are editorial judgments. Claims about firsthand tests should identify the method and supporting evidence; a feature-list comparison is not a controlled performance test.

Writing and illustration tools

We may use AI tools to help draft, edit, and illustrate guides. Illustrations are conceptual artwork, not evidence of product testing or photographs of customers. The use of a tool does not replace the need for accurate sources and clear distinctions between evidence and advice.

Updates and corrections

Publication dates identify when a guide first appeared. Updated dates should reflect substantive changes to the article. Prices and app features can change, so check the linked store or product page before purchasing.

If you find an inaccurate claim or a mismatched citation, contact Productify with the page URL, the passage, and a supporting source. Readers looking for a starting point can browse our habit guides.
