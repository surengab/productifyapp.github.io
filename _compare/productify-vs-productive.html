Productify vs Productive:
Which habit tracker should you choose?

The names are almost identical, but these are two separate apps from two different developers. This page breaks down what each one actually offers (free tier, pricing, accountability, insights) so you can pick the one that fits how you build habits.

Quick Summary

Similar names, different philosophies

Neither app is universally better. Productify and Productive are built around different ideas of what habit tracking should feel like, and they monetize very differently. Here's the short version:

Productify

Calm, accountability-first habit tracking

Built specifically for iPhone. Minimal design, guilt-free streaks, measurable goals, Habit Duo for one-friend accountability, and an AI Habit Analyser for pattern insights. Free to download with no account required.

Productive

Polished, structured, subscription-driven

A well-established habit tracker for iOS and Android with a polished interface. Its signature idea is organizing habits into morning, afternoon, and evening sessions. Strong stats and challenges, but the recurring complaint in reviews is how much of the app sits behind the Premium subscription.

Best for different users

Choose Productify if you…
  • Want a generous free plan with no account required
  • Want to track one habit with a friend (Habit Duo)
  • Track quantities like steps, minutes, or glasses of water
  • Value guilt-free streak tracking that doesn't punish missed days
  • Want AI insights into your habit patterns
  • Prefer a one-time lifetime purchase over subscription-only pricing
Consider Productive if you…
  • Split habits across morning, afternoon, and evening blocks
  • Need an Android version or use both platforms
  • Enjoy challenges alongside other users
  • Like customizable themes, icons, and colors
  • Don't mind paying a subscription for the full feature set

Not sure yet? Productify is free to try with no signup, so the fastest way to decide is to download it and run one habit for a week.

First, the name confusion

If you searched for one of these apps and found the other, you're not alone. Productify and Productive are two unrelated habit trackers with nearly identical names. Productify is an iPhone-first habit tracker focused on calm streaks, shared accountability, and AI insights. Productive (productiveapp.io, developed by Mosaic S.r.l.) is a separate app available on both iOS and Android, best known for organizing habits into time-of-day sessions.

They are made by different companies, have different App Store listings, and take different approaches to pricing. If you came here looking for the free iPhone habit tracker with Habit Duo, that's Productify, and you can get it here. If you came looking for a Productive app alternative, keep reading: the differences below matter.

Design and structure

Productive's signature idea is splitting your day into morning, afternoon, and evening sessions. Instead of a flat habit list, you see blocks of habits tied to parts of the day. If your routine genuinely has that shape (some habits in the morning, different ones at night), it feels more like a plan than a checklist. The interface is polished, with customizable themes, icons, and colors.

Productify takes a more minimal approach. The design is intentionally quiet: fewer visual elements, softer colors, less data on screen at once. If busy interfaces make you less likely to open your habit app, Productify's calmer aesthetic may help. And if all your habits happen at the same time of day anyway, Productive's session structure can become visual noise rather than clarity.

Free tier and pricing

This is the biggest practical difference between the two apps, and the most common theme in Productive's reviews. Productive is free to download, but the free tier caps how many habits you can create, and much of what makes the app appealing (advanced stats, challenges, customization, location reminders) requires Premium. Independent reviews also describe the upgrade prompts as aggressive, appearing on first open and returning regularly. Premium is subscription-based: the App Store listing shows monthly and yearly plans (yearly from $19.99, with pricing that varies by region and offer) and no lifetime unlock at the time of writing.

Productify is free to download with no account required. The free plan includes up to 5 habits, streaks, reminders, and 5 starter templates. Productify Pro (which adds unlimited habits, all templates, custom habit units, Habit Duo and the AI Habit Analyser) costs $3.99 per month, $24.99 per year, or a one-time $34.99 lifetime purchase. If subscription fatigue is what brought you here, the lifetime option is the clearest difference.

Measurable goals and streaks

Productify supports measurable goals: habits where you track quantities like steps, minutes of reading, glasses of water, or reps, with a clear daily progress indicator. Productive's tracking is primarily built around checking habits off within your daily sessions, backed by solid streak stats and completion trends.

Both apps track streaks. The difference is tone. Productify deliberately avoids punitive streak messaging: missing a day resets your current count, but your history stays intact and the app doesn't send guilt-driven warnings. A broken streak is treated as a pause, not a verdict. You can read more about that philosophy on the streak tracking page, or just try it free and miss a day on purpose.

Accountability: challenges vs Habit Duo

Productive offers challenges you can take on alongside other users, which adds a communal, gamified layer of motivation. It works if public momentum motivates you, but it's a broadcast kind of social, not a personal one.

Productify includes Habit Duo (Pro), which lets you pair with one friend to track a single shared habit together. You see each other's progress privately: no leaderboards, no strangers, no competition. Research consistently shows that social accountability is one of the strongest predictors of habit consistency, and Habit Duo is designed to capture that effect quietly, with one person who actually knows you.

On accountability: a global challenge is motivating until you fall behind strangers. A single friend who notices when you skip a day is motivating precisely when you fall behind. That's the bet Habit Duo makes.

Stats vs AI insights

Productive gives you detailed statistics: streaks, completion rates, and habit trends over weeks and months. It presents your data clearly and professionally.

Productify's AI Habit Analyser (Pro) goes a step further by interpreting your data rather than just charting it. It reads your completion history to surface patterns: your most consistent days, time-of-day trends, and which habits rise or fall together. If you've ever stared at a completion-rate chart and wondered what to actually do differently, that interpretive layer is the difference. It ships with the same app you can download free today.

Which one should you choose?

If your habits genuinely split across morning, afternoon, and evening, if you need Android, or if community challenges motivate you, Productive is a polished choice, provided you're comfortable subscribing to unlock most of it. If you want a habit tracker that's genuinely useful for free, tracks measurable goals, offers one-friend accountability and AI insights, and lets you pay once for lifetime access, Productify is built for exactly that.

The honest summary: Productive's most common criticism is its paywall, and Productify's free plan plus $34.99 lifetime option is the direct answer to it. Download Productify free and see if it fits before spending anything.
