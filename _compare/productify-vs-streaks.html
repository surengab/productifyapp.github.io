Productify vs Streaks:
Which habit tracker should you choose?

Streaks is one of the most respected paid habit trackers on iPhone, and Productify is a free alternative with a different philosophy. This page breaks down the real differences, including pricing, the 24-task limit, automatic tracking, and accountability, so you can pick the one that fits how you actually live.

Quick Summary

Two different philosophies

Neither app is universally better. Streaks and Productify are built around different ideas of what a habit tracker should be. Here's the short version:

Productify

Free, accountability-first habit tracking

Built for iPhone with a calm, minimal design. Free to download, no account required, guilt-free streaks, Habit Duo for one-friend accountability, and the TempO AI Habit Analyser for pattern insights. Best for people who want to start free and add Pro features only if they need them.

Streaks

Polished, one-time-purchase Apple ecosystem tracker

An Apple Design Award winner by Crunchy Bagel, sold as a one-time $5.99 purchase with no subscription. Tracks up to 24 daily tasks, connects to the Health app for automatic tracking, and runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro.

Best for different users

Choose Productify if you…
  • Want a free habit tracker you can try before paying anything
  • Want to track one habit with a friend (Habit Duo)
  • Value guilt-free streak tracking that doesn't punish missed days
  • Want AI insights into your habit patterns
  • Prefer a no-account-required setup
  • Want 50+ habit templates to get started immediately
Consider Streaks if you…
  • Prefer a one-time purchase over any subscription
  • Want automatic tracking through the Apple Health app
  • Use iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro alongside your iPhone
  • Track a compact routine (up to 24 tasks per day)
  • Value a widely praised, Apple Design Award winning design

Pricing: one-time purchase vs freemium

This is the clearest difference between the two apps, and for many people it decides the comparison on its own. Streaks costs $5.99 as a one-time purchase on the App Store. There is no subscription and no free version: you pay once, up front, and you own the app. If you dislike subscriptions on principle, that model is genuinely appealing, and Streaks has earned its 4.8-star rating from over 27,000 reviews.

Productify flips the model. The app is free to download with no account required, and core habit tracking, reminders, templates, streaks, and widgets are included. An optional Pro plan ($3.99 per month, $24.99 per year, or $34.99 lifetime) unlocks Habit Duo and the TempO AI Habit Analyser. The practical upshot: with Streaks you commit $5.99 before your first check-in, while with Productify you can build a working routine for free and only pay if the Pro features earn it.

The 24-task limit and habit flexibility

Streaks supports up to 24 tasks per day. For most people building a focused routine, 24 is more than enough, and the constraint arguably encourages restraint. But if your habits span health, work, learning, and home, or you like to rotate seasonal habits in and out, a hard ceiling can start to chafe.

Productify doesn't cap the number of habits you can create, and its 50+ ready-made templates make it fast to add new ones. It also supports measurable goals (habits where you track quantities like steps, minutes, glasses of water, or reps) with a clear daily progress indicator. If you're not sure how many habits you'll end up tracking, starting free with no ceiling is the safer bet.

Apple ecosystem and automatic tracking

Credit where it's due: Streaks is superb here. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro, won an Apple Design Award, and connects to the Apple Health app so tasks like steps, running distance, or heart-rate goals can complete themselves automatically. If hands-off tracking across every Apple device you own is your top priority, Streaks is one of the best apps ever built for that.

Productify is iPhone-first, with an Apple Watch app and home screen widgets, and it asks you to check habits off yourself. That's a deliberate choice: the small moment of manually marking a habit done is part of how the habit sticks. Productify's measurable goals cover quantity-based habits, and the app holds 4.5 stars from 493 App Store ratings. If you mostly live on your iPhone, Productify covers the devices that matter without the extra platforms you may never use.

Accountability: solo streaks vs Habit Duo

Streaks is primarily a personal tracker, though it does let you share tasks with other Streaks users so they can encourage you and keep you accountable. It's a useful option, but accountability is not the app's organizing idea; the streak count is.

Productify puts accountability at the center with Habit Duo: you pair with one friend on a single shared habit, see each other's progress privately, and there are no leaderboards, no competition, and no public rankings. For users who find that having even one person alongside them makes a meaningful difference, a dedicated paired habit is a stronger commitment device than optional sharing.

On accountability: Research consistently shows that social accountability is one of the most reliable predictors of habit consistency. Productify's Habit Duo is designed to capture that effect quietly: one friend, one habit, no pressure.

AI insights and habit analysis

Productify's TempO AI Habit Analyser (available on Pro) reads your completion history to surface patterns: your most consistent days, time-of-day trends, and which habits tend to go together or fall apart together. The insights are calm and informational. They don't offer generic motivational advice, they show you what your own data says.

Streaks focuses on statistics and streak history rather than interpretation, and offers task suggestions when you add new tasks. It presents your data cleanly, but it doesn't include an AI layer that explains your patterns. If understanding why your habits succeed or fail matters to you, that analysis layer is something you can only get on the Productify side of this comparison.

Which one should you choose?

If you want a beautifully polished habit tracker, prefer paying $5.99 once over any subscription, and love the idea of Health app goals completing themselves across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, Streaks is an excellent choice and its reputation is deserved. If you want to start free with no account, track as many habits as you like, share one habit with a friend through Habit Duo, and get AI insights into your patterns, Productify is designed for exactly that.

The clearest signal: if you're hesitant to pay before knowing whether habit tracking will stick for you, download Productify free and find out. You can always buy Streaks later; you can't un-spend the $5.99.
