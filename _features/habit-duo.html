How It Works

Set up in 30 seconds. Keep going for months.

Three steps. That's it.

1

Pick a habit

Choose from habit templates or create your own. Set a measurable goal — steps, minutes, pages, glasses of water, reps. Whatever fits.

2

Invite one friend

Tap invite, type your friend's name, send a link. They accept in one tap — no complicated setup. Both of you are in. You can invite a partner, a sibling, a friend across the country.

Every day, you both see whether the other checked in. Quiet. Private. Just the two of you — with a shared streak that supports you through bad days and celebrates the good ones.

Why doing it together matters

Most people who struggle to build habits aren't lacking willpower — they're lacking a witness. Research on goal accountability suggests that sharing progress with even one person can meaningfully improve follow-through. It's not about pressure. It's about the simple human instinct to not let someone down.

HabitDuo takes this principle and builds it into the app in the quietest, least intrusive way possible. You pick one habit. You invite one person. You both track it independently in your own Productify app. And you can see each other's progress — a calm, private view of whether the other person showed up today.

Most accountability features in apps are built around competition: who has the longer streak, who has more points, who won the week. That model works for some people. But for many — especially those building quieter, personal habits like journaling, sleep routines, or mindfulness — competition adds the wrong kind of pressure. HabitDuo is intentionally non-competitive. No scores, no rankings, no notifications about your partner's lead. Just a shared view of mutual effort.

Works across any distance

HabitDuo doesn't require you to be in the same city — or even the same timezone. A shared habit is something you can both do on your own schedule, in your own space. Many people use it with a friend who moved away, a sibling across the country, or a partner with a different daily routine.
