Your daily record

What does a habit tracking app do?

A habit tracking app records a repeated action against the schedule you choose. Instead of trying to remember whether you practiced this week, you have a record to review. Reminders cue the action; your check-ins show what happened.

Productify supports habits you check off and measurable goals such as minutes, pages, or repetitions. You enter your progress yourself: the app does not automatically detect completed activities.

A one-time errand belongs on a to-do list. A recurring reading session belongs in a habit tracker. Our tracker versus to-do list guide explains how to choose.

From setup to review

How to track a habit in Productify

  1. Name an action. Create your own habit or choose from the habit templates. If you need inspiration, browse 30 habit tracker ideas.
  2. Choose the schedule and target. Decide when the habit is due and what counts as done. For reading, that could be 10 minutes after dinner on weekdays.
  3. Set a reminder. Choose a time when you can act. Adjust it if the reminder repeatedly arrives at an inconvenient moment.
  4. Log the result. Check off the action or enter the quantity you completed. Record partial progress honestly instead of treating the target as already finished.
  5. Review the week. Look for missed scheduled days and adjust the time, goal, or frequency. A smaller repeatable action may fit better than your first plan.

After a missed day: the current streak count resets, while your recorded history stays intact. Resume on the next scheduled day and use the record to adjust the routine. Read more about streaks and completion history.

Find the right fit

Is Productify the right habit tracker for you?

Consider Productify if you want to log a routine on iPhone, use measurable goals, and start without creating an account. If you need Android, a web app, automatic activity detection, or a full to-do manager, compare those requirements before choosing.
